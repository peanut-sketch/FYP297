Where the data comes from

No real panel or meter data is used yet. Green = real input; blue = calculated from real weather; yellow = our assumption; red = illustrative, not measured.

ItemStatusSource and caveat
WeatherRealOpen-Meteo forecast and archive. Coarse resolution; fast Singapore storm clouds are hard to forecast.
Installed capacity (2,339 MWp)RealEnergy Market Authority figure, set in config.json and updated quarterly.
Fleet output, yield, tracker comparisonModelledpvlib physics model. Depends on assumed settings: fleet factor 0.92 (a guess), system loss 0.86 (generic default; measured rooftop values are often lower), 10° south tilt, tracker (rare on Singapore rooftops, carried over from the Edinburgh design).
Machine-learning forecast and accuracyModelledTrained on, and scored against, our physics model (a proxy) because no bench readings exist yet. Real-panel accuracy is unknown.
System demand and solar shareIllustrativeA typical day shape, not EMA demand data. Real data and a demand forecast are planned.
Home and office loads, self-sufficiencyIllustrativeGeneric profiles scaled to 1 kWh a day with a simple 1 kWh battery. A real flat uses roughly 10 kWh a day, so results show the idea, not a Singapore finding.
Fault checkSimulatedFour faults inserted into simulated data and found again. Works on total plant power; per-panel diagnosis needs sensors (planned).
Weather: Open-Meteo. Model: pvlib. Adapted from cindyiskandar/digital-twin-solar-pv.
